# Submission checklist and templates

Copy what is useful. None of this is graded on formatting.

## Checklist

- [ ] `AI-USE.md` present (required, see below)
- [ ] `01-critique.md` or `.pdf`
- [ ] `02-problems.md` or `.pdf` or legible photographs
- [ ] `03-code/` with your `semantic_entropy.py`, `RESULTS.md`, `AUDIT.md`, and tests
- [ ] Every number you report was actually computed, or is marked `[TODO: not computed]`
- [ ] Every paper claim you make cites a specific section, figure, or table
- [ ] `NOTES.md` if you want to record assumptions or what you ran out of time for

Email to **research@substrate-labs.org**, subject `Assessment: <your name>`.

---

## `AI-USE.md` template

```markdown
# AI tool use

Tools used: <e.g. Claude Opus 5, GitHub Copilot, none>

Part 1 (critique):  <what you used it for, roughly how much of the final text>
Part 2 (problems):  <same>
Part 3 (code):      <same>

Anything you leaned on heavily and would want to flag:
<optional>
```

Being specific here does not cost you anything. "Drafted section 3 with a model,
then rewrote it after checking the figure myself and finding the draft had the
direction backwards" is a genuinely good line to be able to write.

---

## `NOTES.md` template

```markdown
# Notes

## Assumptions
<Ambiguities you hit, and how you resolved them.>

## Ran out of time on
<What you would have done next, and how.>

## Things I am unsure about
<Claims you make but are not confident in. Flagging these helps you.>
```

The "unsure about" section is read carefully and it helps rather than hurts.
Calibration is part of what we are assessing.
